// Autonomous Institute

GOVERNMENT COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AURANGABAD

Government College OF Engineering Aurangabad an Autonomous Engineering Institute of Government of Maharashtra is promoting and educating the engineering students with a passion of learning since 1960. The college is well versed with highly qualified staff and good educational facilities. The college also encourages students for higher education and helps them for the same.

At the same time it has pushed the students in the extra-curricular activities as well for the overall development of every student. It organizes various technical events for letting the students have exposure to the practical world. It has a glorious history of 58 years and strives to maintain the same.

https://www.geca.ac.in/

VISION

In pursuit of global competitiveness, the institute is committed to excel in engineering education and research with concern for environment and society.

MISSION

To provide a conductive environment for academic excellence in engineering education. To enhance research and development along with a promotion to sponsored projects and industrial consultancy. To foster the development of students by creating awareness for the need of society, sustainable development and human values.

// Official Dispatches

NEWS & UPDATES

NATIONAL (VIRTUAL) RESULT 2021 SEASON

In 2020, Team Aryans stood at 7th pole position in endurance race and achieved AIR 3. The team qualified for phase two of BAJA SAE India at Pithampur, Indore.

NATIONAL RESULT 2020 SEASON

In 2020, Team Aryans stood at 7th pole position in endurance race and achieved AIR 2 with the podium finish and bagged 1st rank in Suspension and Traction.

VIRTUAL RESULT 2020 SEASON

Virtuals is the first phase of BAJA SAE Competition which is also known as the design round. Artemis achieved the title of being India's First Rockcrawler and the team also bagged first award in the Sales and Marketing event.

VIRTUAL RESULT 2019 SEASON

With a kick start to this season, we secured ALL INDIA RANK 1 in BAJA Virtuals 2020 held at Chitkara University, Chandigarh, qualifying for phase two at Pithampur, Indore.

INTERNATIONAL RESULT 2018 SEASON

For the very first time, Team Aryans participated in BAJA SAE International. The team stood 7th in sales presentation, 10th in acceleration event, 11th in design event and 29th in the endurance race, securing an overall rank of 34 globally.

NATIONAL RESULT (IIT ROPAR) 2018 SEASON

In 2018, Team Aryans secured All India Rank of 2 held at IIT Ropar, Punjab. The team also bagged up three awards: Raftaar Award, Suspension Traction Award, and Acceleration Award.
